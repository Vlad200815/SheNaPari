## 1. Introduction

1.1 Welcome to **Shenapari** — a B2B food-ordering platform that connects users with food vendors and enables menu browsing, in-app ordering, card payment, and QR-code pickup. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use our mobile application, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have over it.

1.2 By creating an account or using the Shenapari app, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy. Your continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1.3 The most current version of this policy is always available at <https://shenapari.me/privacy>.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Information You Provide Directly

3.1.1 When you register or use the app, you provide the following personal data:

- **Full name** — required at registration to identify your orders.
- **Phone number** — your primary account identifier, used to send you a one-time password (OTP) via SMS for authentication.
- **City** — to indicate your location for service relevance.
- **Date of birth** — used for age verification and, where applicable, personalized offers.
- **Gender** — provided at registration for personalization purposes; selection is voluntary.

3.1.2 We do not collect your email address, home address, or national identification number.

### 3.2 Automatically Collected Information

3.2.1 When you use the app, we and our technology partners automatically collect:

- **Device information** — device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers, used for crash diagnostics and push notification delivery.
- **App usage data** — which screens you visit and which features you use, collected in anonymized form via Firebase Analytics.
- **Crash reports** — technical details about app errors, stack traces, and device state at the time of a crash, collected via Firebase Crashlytics.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token** — a unique token generated by your device that allows us to deliver push notifications to you.
- **Order data** — the items you ordered, quantities, unit prices, total amount, order timestamp, and the unique QR code UUID assigned to each order.

### 3.3 Payment Information

3.3.1 Payments are processed through a licensed third-party card payment processor. When you pay by card:

- Your card number, CVV, and expiry date are entered directly on the payment processor's secure page.
- **Shenapari never sees, stores, or transmits your raw card details.**
- We receive only a transaction confirmation token and payment status from the processor.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

4.1 We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

4.1.1 **Account creation and management** — to create and maintain your account using your full name, phone number, city, date of birth, and gender.

4.1.2 **Authentication** — to send an OTP to your phone number and verify your identity. OTP codes are transmitted only and are never stored in your profile.

4.1.3 **Order processing and fulfilment** — to receive, process, and fulfil your orders using order data and QR code UUID.

4.1.4 **Transactional push notifications** — to send you order status updates (confirmed, in preparation, ready for pickup) using your FCM token.

4.1.5 **Marketing communications** — to send you promotional push notifications about new menu items, discounts, and special offers, with your separate explicit consent. You can opt out at any time (see Section 9).

4.1.6 **Payment processing** — your payment is handled entirely by a licensed card payment processor. We do not process raw card data directly.

4.1.7 **App improvement and crash diagnostics** — to diagnose technical failures and improve app quality using crash reports and device information.

4.1.8 **Usage analytics** — to understand how users interact with the app and identify areas for improvement, using anonymized Firebase Analytics data.

4.1.9 **Legal compliance** — to retain order and payment records for the period required by Ukrainian accounting and consumer protection law.

4.2 We do **not** use your data to serve third-party advertisements, sell it to data brokers, or build profiling models for external marketing.

5.2 Where we rely on your **consent**, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

## 7. Data Retention

7.1 We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

- **Full name, phone number, city, date of birth, gender (active account)** — retained until you delete your account.
- **JWT tokens** — retained until expiry or account deletion; stored only on your device, never on our servers.
- **Order history and payment records** — retained for **3 years** (legal obligation under Ukrainian accounting law).
- **Firebase Analytics events** — retained for **26 months** (Google's default; data is anonymized before retention).
- **Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)** — retained for **90 days**.
- **FCM tokens** — refreshed automatically by the device; invalidated upon account deletion.

7.2 Inactive accounts with no login for **24 months** will receive an SMS notification. If no response is received within **30 calendar days**, the account and associated personal data will be deleted.

7.3 You may request deletion of your data at any time using the **"Delete Profile"** function in the app or by emailing supportshenapari@gmail.com. We will process deletion requests within **30 calendar days**.

## 12. Data Security

12.1 We take the security of your personal data seriously:

- **Authentication tokens** (JWT access and refresh tokens) are stored in your device's hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) via `flutter_secure_storage`. They are never written to shared preferences or logs.
- **Data in transit** is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher between the app and our servers.
- **Server-side data** is hosted on Microsoft Azure with access controls, encryption at rest, and network isolation.
- **OTP codes** are generated server-side, stored temporarily in Redis with a 60-second TTL, and deleted immediately after verification. They are never stored in your profile.

12.2 No security system is impenetrable. In the event of a data breach that poses a significant risk to your rights, we will notify affected users and, where required by law, the relevant supervisory authority **within 72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach.
