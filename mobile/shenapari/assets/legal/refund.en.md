## 1. General provisions

1.1 These Terms explain when and how money for an order in the **SheNaPari** app is refunded. They add to the **Terms of Use**.

1.2 Payment in the current version of the App is **simulated**: no real money is charged, so refunds happen only inside the App. The rules below will stay the same once real payment is added.

## 2. When you get a full refund

2.1 **You cancelled the order** before the kitchen started cooking it (status «Paid» or «Accepted»). The «Cancel order» button is on the order screen.

2.2 **The kitchen cancelled the order**, for example because a dish ran out. You will see the reason in the order and in the notification.

2.3 In both cases the **full amount** of the order is refunded automatically, in the same way you paid (Apple Pay or Google Pay). You don't need to ask for it.

## 3. When money is not refunded

3.1 **The order is already being cooked.** Once cooking has started, it cannot be cancelled.

3.2 **The order was not picked up.** A ready order waits for **30 minutes**; if nobody picks it up, it is closed as «not picked up» and the money is not refunded, because the food has already been cooked.

## 4. Timing

4.1 In the App, the payment status changes right after the cancellation.

4.2 Once real payment is added, how long the money takes to reach your card will depend on your bank, usually up to a few business days.

## 5. If something went wrong

If you think you should have been refunded, write to support in the App: **More → Support**, or to [supportshenapari@gmail.com](mailto:supportshenapari@gmail.com), and give the order number. We will reply within 3 business days.
