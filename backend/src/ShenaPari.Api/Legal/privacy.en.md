## 1. General provisions

1.1 **SheNaPari** is an app for ordering food in the university canteen: you browse the menu, order for the next break and pick the order up by QR code without queuing.

1.2 This Policy explains which personal data we process, why, how long we keep it and what rights you have. By using the app, you confirm that you have read it.

## 2. Who we are

2.1 The app is developed and maintained by an individual developer (the **«Operator»**) under the name SheNaPari.

2.2 Contact: [supportshenapari@gmail.com](mailto:supportshenapari@gmail.com).

## 3. What data we process

### 3.1 Data you give us

- **Phone number**: your login. The one-time sign-in code is sent to it.
- **Name**: the kitchen calls it out when your order is ready.

### 3.2 Data that appears while you use the app

- **Orders**: dishes, total, chosen break, status and the time of each status change.
- **Technical sign-in data**: session tokens, sign-in time and the IP address in server logs.
- **Push notification token** (Android only), to tell you when your order status changes.

### 3.3 What we do not collect

Payment in the app is currently **simulated**: we do not receive or store bank card details. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, date of birth or gender.

## 4. Why we need this data

- signing in to your account and protecting it from others;
- taking, cooking and handing over orders;
- telling you the status of your order;
- the order history in your profile;
- technical support, finding bugs and preventing abuse.

We do **not** use the data for advertising and do not sell it.

## 5. Who sees your data

- **Canteen staff** (cooks and administrators) see your name and what you ordered, only to cook and hand it over.
- **Technical service providers**: server hosting, the SMS service for sign-in codes, Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) for push notifications. They get only what they need to do their job.
- **Public authorities**, only where the law directly requires it.

## 6. How long we keep data

- Account and orders: while the account exists.
- One-time sign-in codes: 5 minutes.
- Session tokens: until you sign out or they expire.
- Server logs: up to 30 days.

When you delete your account, your name and phone number are erased at once and all sessions end. Orders stay in the canteen's records but are no longer linked to anyone.

## 7. Your rights

You can:

- find out what data about you is stored and get a copy of it;
- correct your name in the profile;
- delete your account: **More → My profile → Settings → Delete account** (if you have no active order);
- withdraw consent to push notifications in your phone's settings.

For other requests, write to [supportshenapari@gmail.com](mailto:supportshenapari@gmail.com) and give the phone number of your account. We will reply within 30 days.

## 8. Security

Data is sent over an encrypted connection, tokens are kept in the phone's secure storage (Keychain / Keystore), and staff access is limited by roles.

## 9. Minors

The app is meant for university students and staff. If you are under 16, use it only with your parents' consent.

## 10. Changes to the Policy

We may update this Policy. The current version is always available in the app; we will let you know separately about significant changes.
