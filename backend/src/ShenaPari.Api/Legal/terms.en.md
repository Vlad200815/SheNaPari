## 4. What the App does

- shows the canteen menu and which dishes are available;
- takes orders for a chosen break or «as soon as possible»;
- tells you the status of your order;
- shows a QR code to pick it up.

The canteen sets the menu, prices and break schedule and may change them at any time.

## 5. Orders and payment

5.1 An order is accepted once the kitchen has confirmed it and set the ready time.

5.2 The kitchen may pause taking orders for a while; new orders cannot be placed then.

5.3 Payment in the current version of the App is **simulated**: no real money is charged and no card details are requested.

## 6. Picking up an order

6.1 A ready order is handed over by the QR code in the App. Show it to the canteen staff.

6.2 An order not picked up within **30 minutes** of becoming «Ready» is closed automatically.

## 7. Cancellation

You can cancel an order until the kitchen starts cooking it. Once cooking has started, it cannot be cancelled.
